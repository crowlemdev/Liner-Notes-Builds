For Mac

Liner Notes for Mac

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon

  1. Open the zip and drag Liner Notes into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS stops it the first time, as it does any app Apple has not checked.
  3. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway. From then on, updates install themselves.

For Android

Liner Notes for Android

Android 9 or later · phones and music players

  1. Download the APK on your phone or player.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, let your browser install unknown apps, then choose Install.
  3. From then on, new builds install from inside the app.

In the sleeve

The signal

Every track states how it reaches you, in words and numbers. Bit-perfect over USB on Android, and with exclusive access on the Mac.

  • Lossless
  • Enhanced
  • High quality
  • Low quality

The pages

Albums, artists, Serendipity and Stats, set like print: serif names, generous margins and the art first.

The playlists

Plain M3U files in a folder you choose, tagged by genre. Sync the folder, and your Mac and your player share them.
